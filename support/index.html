Common questions

I forgot my password

On the sign-in screen, tap Forgot your password? and enter your email. We'll email you a code to set a new one. If it doesn't arrive, check your spam folder.

My reminders don't ring

Check that notifications are allowed for Over Coffee in your phone's settings, then open Settings → Reminders in the app. If the app was installed a while ago, updating it from the App Store or Google Play may also be needed.

How do I send someone a task?

First connect with them in Settings → People using their email address. They need an Over Coffee account and have to approve the request. After that, open any task and choose them to hand it over. They can finish it or hand it back.

The pop-up about overdue tasks is too much

Turn it off in Settings → Nudges. Your overdue tasks stay on your list. It just stops asking.

Can I add tasks by talking to Claude?

Yes. In Settings → Connect Claude, make your private link and add it to Claude as a connector. Anything Claude adds waits for you to review before it joins your list.

How do I delete my account?

In the app, go to Settings → Delete account and type DELETE to confirm. Everything in your account is deleted straight away, and it can't be undone. If you can't get into the app, email us from the address on your account and we'll do it for you.

What do you do with my information?

Only what's needed to run the app. No ads, no analytics, no tracking. The privacy policy has the details.
